# PlayLog

## Design Context

### Users
Gamers who play across multiple storefronts and consoles and want one place to log what they've played, how long, and how good it was. Used casually — evenings, between sessions, often right after finishing a game. The job: "log this game before I forget, and let me browse my history like a trophy shelf."

### Brand Personality
Loud, competitive, arcade-slick. The energy of a fighting-game character-select screen or a Persona 5 menu — confident, kinetic, a little aggressive. Never corporate, never pastel, never soft SaaS.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Dark theme (used at night, next to a running game; dark is the correct context call)
- Near-black ink surfaces tinted toward red, one crimson-red accent used sparingly but hard
- Diagonal energy: clip-path slabs, skewed chips, -2° tilts, striped hazard textures
- Display type: Anton (condensed poster caps). Body/UI: Barlow
- Ratings are S–E tier letters, set huge, like an arcade grade screen
- Anti-references: generic SaaS landing pages, glassmorphism, purple-blue gradients

### Design Principles
1. One accent, used like a weapon — red appears where action or grade lives, nowhere else
2. Diagonals carry the brand — straight boxes are the exception, not the rule
3. Type does the decorating — huge condensed caps instead of icons and illustrations
4. The dashboard is a shelf, not a spreadsheet — covers first, chrome minimal
5. Dark surfaces get depth from lightness steps, never from glow or blur
